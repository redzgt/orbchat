# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People using a compact room-based messaging widget embedded in Orb Games, with the option to open the same interface as a standalone page.

## Product Purpose

OrbChat lets people create or join a temporary room with a short code and exchange messages in real time. Success means the interface remains immediate, lightweight, and understandable inside a small embed.

## Positioning

An ultra-simple, no-account room experience whose history is temporary and cleared after the room empties.

## Operating Context

The primary context is an embed within orb.games. The page must also remain complete and usable when opened directly on desktop or mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve room creation, six-character room joining, real-time WebSocket messaging, connection state, and automatic room cleanup.
- Preserve the plain Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects implementation.
- Keep the interface compact, responsive, accessible, and free of unnecessary navigation or onboarding.
- Metadata and labels must describe the product honestly.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the supplied OrbChat wordmark as the primary logo asset.
- Visually belong to the same dark navy, electric-blue, rounded interface system as orb.games.
- Keep the product name OrbChat.

## Evidence on Hand

- Supplied wordmark image.
- Existing working room and messaging implementation.
- orb.games is the binding visual reference.

## Product Principles

- Enter or create a room with minimal friction.
- Preserve the small embeddable footprint.
- Match the Orb Games family without copying unnecessary site chrome.
- Keep connection and error states clear.
